Tuesday: Addition & Subtraction
Mathematics

Goal: understand addition as putting together and subtraction as taking away.

  1. 3 min: Put out 5 Lego blocks. Add 2. Ask “How many altogether?”
  2. 5 min: Solve 2+1, 3+2, 4+1 and 5+3 using counters or fingers.
  3. 5 min: Solve 4−1, 5−2, 6−3 and 8−2 by physically taking objects away.
  4. 4 min: Story: “5 ducks are swimming. 2 more arrive. How many now?” Then “8 cars are parked. 3 drive away. How many remain?”
  5. 2 min: Child explains one answer in their own words.
